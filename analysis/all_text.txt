SafeNet Security Services | #1 Private Security Company in Ghana
SafeNet Security Services Ltd is a licensed and registered private security company based in Adjirigano, Accra, Ghana. We are committed to delivering reliable, professional, and technology-enhanced security services to individuals, residential communities, commercial entities, institutions, and government agencies.
At SafeNet, we believe security is more than presence — it is trust, preparedness, and proactive risk management. That’s why every engagement begins with a comprehensive risk assessment, enabling us to design tailored solutions that align with our clients’ specific environments and industries.
From deploying trained guards and mobile patrols to managing high-profile events, monitoring surveillance systems, and responding to emergencies, we deliver peace of mind backed by integrity, vigilance, and client satisfaction.
Our Vision
To become the most trusted and technologically advanced private security provider in Ghana and West Africa, known for integrity, rapid response, and exceptional client service.
Our Mission
To deliver high-quality, customized security solutions that protect lives, assets, and reputations through disciplined personnel, advanced technology, and unmatched client service.
our values
Integrity
We operate with honesty, transparency, and accountability. Trust is the cornerstone of our client relationships.
Professionalism
From appearance and conduct to reporting and response, we hold our guards and staff to the highest standards of discipline and efficiency
Vigilance
To deliver high-quality, customized security solutions that protect lives, assets, and reputations through disciplined personnel, advanced technology, and unmatched client service
Client-Centered Service
Every decision we make revolves around the clients needs. We listen, adapt, and deliver with precision and care.
Innovation
We embrace new technologies and smart security tools to deliver modern, effective solutions.
Respect
At our core, we are dedicated to delivering trusted security and surveillance solutions that provide peace of mind for homes and businesses alike. Our expertise spans from the installation of advanced CCTV systems.

We treat every client, employee, and community with dignity and fairness, regardless of status or background.
